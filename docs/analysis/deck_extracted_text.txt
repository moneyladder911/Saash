It is where the company is domiciled, where fulfilment is cheapest, and where Chalhoub Group, owner of Sephora Middle East and Faces, controls close to half the

Hardware sensing
Sensors in the controller measure hydration and sebum directly from the skin 
surface. Because the reading is taken from the skin rather than inferred from 
an image, it holds across every skin tone.

02
Image analysis
The application reads redness, hyperpigmentation and texture from the 
camera and adjusts treatment zone by zone across the face. It runs 
alongside the sensor, not in place of it.
TWO INDEPENDENT PATHS, ONE ADAPTIVE PROTOCOL
ONE RECORD, ONE ACCOUNT
Every session is written to a single customer identity shared by the website, the application and every device. The skin record starts on the first use and compounds 
from there. It is the asset the hardware exists to create.
Most AI in this category is a photo filter with a marketing budget. Ours is an instrument with a laboratory reference behind it.
Sensing method and implementation are the subject of pending protection. The application is owned outright by the UAE entity, in beta today, with production release ahead of launch.

DEFENSIBILITY
Why a larger company cannot simply add this.
10CONFIDENTIAL
01
It is a hardware decision
The sensors sit in the controller, against the skin. 
No competitor can add measurement in a 
software update. It requires re-tooling, re-
certification and a new production run. That is a 
product cycle, not a sprint.
02
Melanin-independence is built in
The reading comes from the skin surface rather 
than from a camera image, so accuracy does not 
degrade on darker skin. A camera-based 
competitor cannot correct its way to the same 
result, because the limitation is in the input.
03
The record compounds
Every session is stored from the first use. A 
customer eighteen months into their skin history 
holds data a new entrant cannot backfill, and will 
not abandon to start again elsewhere.
Validated across Fitzpatrick I–VI against the Courage + Khazaka laboratory instrument, the German standard.

WHY THE FIRST ORDER DOES NOT NEED TO BE LUCRATIVE
It only needs to be positive, because it is the only order that carries an acquisition cost. Every order after it is sold to a customer the company already owns, so 
the whole contribution drops through.
That is the reason for the price ladder. The entry device buys the customer; the portfolio is where the money is made.

# LAYL — Image Prompt Pack

Photo-real campaign images for Collection Nº 01 *After Dark*. Use these in any AI image
generator, or hand them to a photographer as the shoot brief. Each look matches a page in
`LAYL_Maison_Lookbook.pdf`.

**How to use**
1. Paste the **House style** block at the end of every prompt, so all images look like one campaign.
2. Generate 4–8 variations per look and keep the best 1–2. Portrait **4:5** for Instagram and the lookbook, **9:16** for stories and TikTok, **16:9** for the website hero.
3. Re-upload your best image as a style reference so the rest of the set matches it.
4. AI tools often get logos and text wrong. Add the LAYL crescent and any lettering afterwards in Photoshop or Canva.
5. Before using an AI image in paid ads, check the image tool's commercial-use terms. Before launch, replace AI images with photos of real samples.

## House style (append to every prompt)

> luxury sportswear campaign, shot at night on an outdoor padel court in Dubai, glass walls and floodlights, deep
> navy night sky, warm skyline bokeh in the far background, cinematic low-key lighting with a single hard floodlight
> from above and soft fill, rich blacks, subtle film grain, editorial fashion photography, shot on medium format,
> 85mm lens, shallow depth of field, muted palette of midnight navy, onyx, sand, ivory, café brown and deep cherry,
> one thin neon-lime reflective line catching the light, quiet luxury, no visible brand logos, no text

**Negative / avoid:** cartoon, illustration, 3D render look, plastic skin, oversaturated neon, busy background,
sportswear catalogue lighting, big logos, text, watermark, extra fingers, distorted rackets.

## Real photoshoot brief (once samples exist)

- **When:** 9pm–1am, on a real outdoor court. Book 2 nights: one clear night, one with a little haze.
- **Light:** the court's own floodlights as the key light, plus one soft fill. Add a smoke machine for atmosphere.
- **Cast:** 2 women (one wearing the modest look), 2 men, 1 couple. Real players, so the movement looks right.
- **Shot list per look:** full length (4:5), in motion, one detail close-up, one court-to-dinner lifestyle shot.
- **Deliverables:** 40 final stills, 10 vertical videos (6–15s), 1 hero film (30–45s) for the waitlist page and launch ads.
